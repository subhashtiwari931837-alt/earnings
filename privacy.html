Earnings & Payment

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

This page explains what information the Earnings & Payment app collects, why, and how it is kept. The app is run by Subhash Tiwari for people working with our freelance group.

What we collect

  • From your Google account, when you sign in: your name, email address and profile picture. We do not get your password or access to anything else in your Google account.
  • What you enter in the form: your Outlier ID, total earned amount, amount paying and who invited you.
  • Screenshots you upload: your earnings page, referral page and payment confirmation.

Why we collect it

  • To record your weekly earnings and the fee you pay.
  • To check payments and keep a record of them.
  • To email you about your entries, for example when a payment is confirmed or an entry needs to be corrected.

Where it is stored

Your details are stored in a private Google Sheet, and your screenshots in a private Google Drive folder, both owned by Subhash Tiwari. Only the app owner can see them. The person you choose under "Invited by" may be told about your payment so they can confirm they received it.

Sharing

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with anyone outside the group except where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your entries for as long as you work with the group and as needed for our payment records.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete your information at any time. You can also remove the app's access to your Google account at myaccount.google.com/connections.
